How to Play Woodoku

Play Woodoku, the addictive wood block puzzle game that combines Tetris and Sudoku. Place wooden blocks strategically to clear lines and columns.

How to Play:

  1. Drag and drop blocks onto the game board
  2. Create complete horizontal or vertical lines to clear them
  3. Clear multiple lines at once for bonus points
  4. Game ends when you can't place any more blocks
  5. Try to beat your high score!

Woodoku combines block-puzzle placement with a 9x9 Sudoku-style grid.

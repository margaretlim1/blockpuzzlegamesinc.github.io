Block Puzzle - Free Online Puzzle Game

How to Play Block Puzzle

Block Puzzle is an addictive puzzle game where you strategically place blocks on a grid to clear lines and score points. Similar to classic tetris-style games, Block Puzzle challenges your spatial reasoning and quick thinking.

Game Features:

  • Free to play online - no download required
  • Relaxing gameplay with no time pressure
  • Beautiful graphics and smooth animations
  • Track your high scores and compete with friends
  • Works on all devices - desktop, tablet, and mobile

How to Play:

  1. Drag and drop blocks onto the game board
  2. Create complete horizontal or vertical lines to clear them
  3. Clear multiple lines at once for bonus points
  4. Game ends when you can't place any more blocks
  5. Try to beat your high score!

Block Puzzle is perfect for quick gaming sessions or extended play. Whether you're looking to kill time, exercise your brain, or just relax, Block Puzzle provides endless entertainment.
